# GameLiftEat — Brand File

> **Status: ACTIVE.** Brand identity contract and operational guidelines.

## Identity & Core Focus

- **Domain:** gamelifteat.com
- **Positioning:** GameLiftEat promotes the fun of cooking, creative healthy eating, and an encouraging lifestyle blending gaming, food, and strength training.
- **Core Pillars:**
  - **Fun Cooking & Fueling:** Creative, delicious recipes, meal prep hacks, and nutrition that tastes great and fuels peak energy.
  - **Approachable Workflows & Strength:** Introducing workouts and strength-building programs in a way that is friendly, encouraging, fun, and subtly nerdy.
  - **Gamer & Foodie Lifestyle:** Health habits, energy optimization, product/supplement reviews, and community challenges tailored for gamers, foodies, and lifters.
- **Brand Mascot:** **Giga**, the Venezuelan Oso Frontino (Spectacled Bear) — GameLiftEat's #1 fan, fictional mascot, and recurring community character appearing in content, memes, and community spotlights.
- **Primary Tagline:** "Level Up Your Lifts. Dominate Your Goals."
- **Visual Palette:** Chinese Black `#141414` · Cultured White `#F7F7F7` · Princeton Orange `#ED7D27`; geometric sans-serif with pixel/gaming accents.

## Audience

Gamers, foodies, desk athletes, and fitness enthusiasts (18–45) looking for practical, fun, and evidence-respecting guidance on food, health, and strength without shame or bro-science.

## Voice

*Practical + energetic + gaming-native + approachable + encouraging.*
Archetype: supportive coach + relatable teammate. Gamer slang and trending meme references used naturally. Never shame-based or dry.

## Content Pillars & Formats

1. **Player's Pantry:** Recipes, speedrun meal prep, creative healthy food swaps.
2. **Approachable Lifts:** Friendly workout intros, strength quests, and beginner-friendly training programs.
3. **Energy & Lifestyle:** Focus, sleep, product reviews (gear, tech, snacks), and habit systems.
4. **Community & Quests:** Founder journey, community challenges, and success spotlights.

## Watch-Topics (feeds `/trend-watch`)

`gaming nutrition`, `fun healthy recipes`, `gamer meal prep`, `approachable workout programs`, `energy drinks & supplement reviews`, `gamer sleep and focus`, `esports fitness`

## Launch Channels & Multi-Channel Growth

- **Multi-Channel Launch:**
  - **Blog (gamelifteat.com):** SEO articles, detailed recipes, and workout guides.
  - **Short-Form Video (TikTok & YouTube Shorts):** Recipe speedruns, quick fitness hacks, product reviews.
  - **Instagram:** Visual meal prep, infographics, exercise tips, community highlights.
- **KPIs:** Published posts/videos per week, organic sessions, social engagement, video views, affiliate revenue.

## Monetization Path

Affiliate product reviews (supplements, kitchen gear, fitness equipment) → digital guides & cookbooks → display ads / brand collabs.
