## Scope resolution: GLE vs GPG (binding — Sentinel enforces)

The vault manual framed GLE as the umbrella gamer-lifestyle brand; the old skeleton framed it as eating-only. **Resolution:** GLE is the gamer **food + exercise lifestyle** brand; GPG is the **training** brand. The checkable line:

| Belongs to GLE | Belongs to GPG |
|---|---|
| Food, recipes, fueling, supplements-vs-evidence | Programs, routines, sets/reps, progression |
| Energy, focus, sleep, caffeine strategy | Exercise technique and how-tos |
| Habit systems, streaks, "why lift as a gamer" motivation | Training gear reviews |
| Community stories, founder journey | Muscle/performance deep-dives |

The manual's **Workout Nexus** hub is therefore parked at lifestyle depth inside GLE; anything crossing into programming/technique routes to GPG. ⚠️ This is the one real override of the manual — Pedro confirms.

## Audience

Gamers (PC/console, 18–35) who lift or want to start; desk athletes optimizing energy and focus for long sessions without trashing their health. They want practical + fun, not shame or bro-science.

## Voice (manual formula)

*Practical + energetic + gaming-native + evidence-respecting.* Archetype: motivating coach + relatable teammate. Gamer metaphors (XP, buffs, debuffs, quests, loadouts, respawn, streaks) used naturally. Every claim sourceable. Never: miracle-supplement energy, shame-based fitness talk, forced memes.
