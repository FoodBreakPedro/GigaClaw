# Brand Identity & Content Guidelines — Karalun Gaming

This file defines the shared brand context, target audience, value proposition, and publishing principles for Karalun Gaming, the SWGOH theorycraft venture.

## Core Brand Identity

**Source:** Grounded in `/Users/pedrozabala/Documents/Development/Github Repos/ZabsAIOS/ventures/karalungaming/brand.md` (v1 active, 2026-07-02) and Obsidian Vault `50-KaralunGaming/index.md`.

- **Brand Name**: Karalun
- **Mission**: Deliver precision planning tools and evidence-backed strategy for Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes players, enabling smarter progression and spending decisions.
- **Target Audience**: Mid-to-endgame SWGOH players (both spenders and F2P/light-spenders) planning era progression. They live on r/SWGalaxyOfHeroes, community Discords, and swgoh.gg. They reward accuracy and generosity; they punish clickbait.
- **Canonical domain**: `planner.gamelifteat.com` (free subdomain on GLE, per Pedro's 2026-07-02 call; dedicated domain only if traction justifies it)
- **Target locales**: None (English primary; localization is [FILL] — no other language tiers planned at launch)
- **Default niche**: `gaming` (SWGOH era planning specific)

## Key Content Pillars

1. **Era Planning Guides**: Per-era leveling paths and checklists, each anchored to the Karalun's Era Planner tool. Actionable, math-backed recommendations.
2. **Spend-Efficiency Analysis**: Crystal pack and bundle value math. The Planner models $4.99–$99.99 packs; content explains which are actually worth buying and why.
3. **Tool Updates & Community Help**: Changelogs, community feature requests, answering roster questions in threads and Discords. The brand grows through generosity.

## Publishing Rules

- **Brand separation (binding)**: Karalun is SWGOH-only. No GameLiftEat or GamePowerGym cross-bleed (Sentinel enforces this per ZabsAIOS brand contracts). No nutrition/training content.
- **Approval gate**: Every public post (Reddit, Discord announcements, video) is an approval item. Pedro posts from his own accounts. Content must be factual and community-first.
- **The Planner stays free**: No paywalled math. Monetization is deferred until meaningful traction (~1k monthly tool users or ~500 followers on any platform); then Ko-fi/Patreon "keep the Planner running", EA Creator Network application.
- **Community conduct**: Value-first, never post-and-ghost. Never drop the tool as a drive-by link — always in context of answering a real question or a substantive guide.
- **Source all claims**: Every recommendation backed by swgoh.gg era pages, in-game pricing, or the Planner's own models.

## Positioning

> *Plan smarter, spend smarter — math and tools for Galaxy of Heroes.*

The signature asset is **Karalun's Era Planner** — a free, open web tool that optimizes era leveling and crystal-pack spending by computing the cheapest mix of packs/bundles to hit player-defined targets. This is the differentiator: every other SWGOH creator grinds video; Karalun ships calculators.

### Game Focus

SWGOH era progression, currently New Republic (ERA08), expanding era-by-era with the game's roadmap. Content follows the live game state; see `WATCH_TOPICS` below.

## Voice & Tone

The guildmate who does the math. Characteristics:
- **Precise**: Claims are never vague or hedged without saying why.
- **Generous**: Explanations are complete; calculations are shown. No gatekeeping.
- **Zero drama**: No FOMO-farming, no "the game is dying" discourse, no drama-chasing.
- **Actionable**: Every conclusion ends with a clear recommendation: "buy X, skip Y, here's the math."
- **Star Wars flavor welcome**: Puns, lore nods, and enthusiasm for the IP are on-brand. Just never let them substitute for accuracy.
