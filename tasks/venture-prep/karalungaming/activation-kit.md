# Karalun — Activation Kit

> Operational guide for Karalun streamer and pop-culture creator channel launch.

## Launch Sequence

1. **Standalone Creator Site:** Setup `karalungaming.com` / `karalun.com` landing page linking Twitch stream schedule, social channels, and Discord.
2. **Twitch Channel Branding & Setup:** Panel overlays, stream schedule (gaming + pop culture discussions), chat bot setup.
3. **Short-Form Content Engine:** Clip stream highlights, game reviews, and pop culture (Star Wars / Marvel / TV) reactions into TikTok, Shorts, and Reels.
4. **Community Discord:** Launch Discord server with channels for game strategy, TV/movie discussions, stream alerts, and community gaming.

## First Content & Stream Seeds

1. **Twitch Live:** Debut stream — PC/Console gaming session + Star Wars/Marvel trailer reaction & community Q&A.
2. **YouTube Shorts / TikTok:** "Top 3 details you missed in the latest Star Wars trailer."
3. **YouTube Video:** "Honest review of [Trending TV Show / Game release] from a gamer's perspective."
4. **Instagram Reel:** Setup tour / stream clip highlight.
5. **Discord Event:** Community gaming night / watch party.
