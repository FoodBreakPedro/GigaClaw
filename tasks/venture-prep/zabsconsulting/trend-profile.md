# ZabsConsulting Trend Profile

Feeds the nightly Night Research workflow. Grounded in
`ventures/zabsconsulting/brand.md` and
`ventures/zabsconsulting/offer-ai-visibility-audit.md`. ZabsConsulting
is the umbrella/revenue engine: AI + automation consulting with a
flagship wedge in AI-results ranking (AEO — being found by AI answers
instead of fighting saturated SEO), building toward authority in AI
news and innovation.

## Keywords / Queries

- AEO / GEO / AI search optimization
- how AI assistants cite sources / answer-engine ranking
- new LLM release business impact
- AI agent tooling for small business
- workflow automation trends (n8n, Make, Zapier)
- AI adoption SMB case study
- AI visibility audit / brand presence in AI answers
- prompt-to-production agent frameworks

## Sources to Watch

- Model-provider releases and changelogs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) — client-impacting capability news is the authority backbone.
- AEO/GEO practitioner discourse: how AI answers select and cite sources; measurement tools for AI visibility.
- Automation-platform ecosystems (n8n releases, template marketplaces) — direct service-delivery signal.
- AI-education creator space (the Julian Goldie / Nate Herk / Tina Huang lane): watch positioning and offer framing among the models Pedro tracks.
- SMB-focused AI adoption surveys and case studies — proof material for consulting offers.

## Competitors

- AI-automation agencies and creator-led consultancies: offer structure and pricing signal — differentiation is the AEO wedge plus a real operating portfolio as proof.
- SEO agencies bolting on "AI optimization": watch their claims; the counter-position is native AEO expertise, not retrofitted SEO.
- AI newsletter/content brands: authority-lane competition for the AI-news positioning.

## Excluded Topics

- Anything touching client-confidential material or Medtronic (day-job separation, hard boundary).
- Political dimensions of AI regulation beyond practical compliance impact for SMB clients.
- Hype-cycle speculation with no actionable business angle ("AGI next year" discourse).
- Crypto/web3 crossover content — off-positioning.

## Content Angle

Practitioner authority: every piece shows the operator's view — what a
new model, tool, or AEO shift means a business should do differently
this quarter. The portfolio ventures double as living case studies
("the AI runs my brands" proof lane). An item scores well when it
converts to a concrete client-facing insight or audit angle, not when
it is merely AI news.

## Cadence Notes

Model releases and tool launches are time-sensitive — 7-day raw window,
covered fast while discourse is forming. AEO methodology and adoption
signal is evergreen within the 30-day window and compounds into the
audit offer. Flag (don't auto-run) anything that would name a real
client or prospect; surfaced in the weekly rollup for Pedro's call.
