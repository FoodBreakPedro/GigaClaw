## Identity

- **One-liner:** *Solo founder running a portfolio of real businesses with an AI-agent operating system he built — and showing the work.*

Not an AI guru selling automation; the actual customer of his own system. The findable person behind Hyperlane Travels, GameLiftEat, GamePowerGym, and Karalun.

## Audience

1. Builders and indie hackers working with AI agents (largest, most engaged audience for build-in-public content).
2. Future ZabsConsulting clients — small-business owners who want what ZabsAIOS does (this brand is ZC's Phase 3 lead engine).
3. Potential partners/collaborators for the ventures.

## Voice

Plain-spoken engineer. Show the artifact, cite the number, admit the failure. The credibility play is honesty about what broke (the 401s, the dashboard detour, the $50 budget) — most AI-automation content is hype from people selling it; this is the unglamorous practitioner's log. Never: growth-hack bravado, "🚀", engagement-bait questions.

## Content pillars

1. **Building ZabsAIOS in public** — weekly "what my agents did this week" digest, auto-drafted from morning briefings, Sentinel-scrubbed, Pedro-approved. The flagship series; near-zero marginal effort.
2. **Ops rigor for AI agents** — approval gates, monitoring, budgets, verification: MedTech thinking applied to agents. The differentiated expertise angle.
3. **Venture build logs** — launches, real metrics, lessons from Hyperlane/GLE/GPG/Karalun as they grow.

## Operational rules (binding)

- Every outbound post/profile update is an approval item; Pedro publishes or explicitly approves. No exceptions (Hard Rule 2).
- Sentinel scrubs every piece for the privacy boundary: nothing from `90-Personal/`, no family details/photos without explicit approval, no employer specifics (see ⚠️ above), no client-identifying Hyperlane facts.
- Venture financials appear only at the granularity Pedro has already approved for public use.
