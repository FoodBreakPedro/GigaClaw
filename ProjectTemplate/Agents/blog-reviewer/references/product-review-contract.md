# Product Review Contract

A Product Review must satisfy all of the following before it can SHIP:

- explain what was evaluated and the evaluation method;
- distinguish direct experience from cited third-party evidence;
- contain material pros, cons, limitations, price/value context, and meaningful alternatives;
- say who the product is and is not for;
- disclose affiliate, sponsorship, or supplied-product relationships;
- avoid implying hands-on testing that the evidence cannot establish;
- use the reviewer-validated product-review CMS category.

Treat invented testing, unsupported specifications, or a missing material relationship disclosure as
P0 trust failures. A generic article with a review-shaped title cannot pass as a Product Review.
