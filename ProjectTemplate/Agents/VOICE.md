# Author Voice & Editorial Style Profile

This file governs tone, sentence structure, pacing, and vocabulary constraints for all content creation and editing.

## Tone & Voice Profile

- **Tone**: Authoritative, concise, engaging, and direct.
- **Pacing**: Varied sentence lengths (high burstiness). Combine punchy short sentences with explanatory technical sentences.
- **Perspective**: Direct second person ("you") or practical first person ("we"). Avoid passive voice.

## Banned AI Clichés & Buzzwords

Do NOT use the following phrases or clichés in articles unless explicitly discussing them as concepts:
- "In today's digital landscape" / "In the ever-evolving world of"
- "It's important to note" / "At its core"
- "Dive into" / "deep dive"
- "Game-changer" / "revolutionize" / "paradigm shift"
- "Seamlessly" / "seamless integration"
- "Empower" / "harness the power of"
- "Tapestry" / "rich tapestry"
- "Leverage" (as a general verb; use "use", "apply", or "build on")

## Readability & Structure Targets

- **Flesch Reading Ease Score**: Target 60–70 (accessible to general professional readers).
- **Paragraph Length**: 2–4 sentences max per paragraph. Avoid walls of text.
- **Headers**: Descriptive, query-focused `##` and `###` headers.
- **Lists & Tables**: Use markdown tables for comparisons and bulleted lists for multi-step processes.
